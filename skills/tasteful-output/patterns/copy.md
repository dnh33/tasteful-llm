# Copy patterns, C1 through C9

9 patterns where headlines, tone and rhetorical choices create slop, grouped by root disease. The examples are illustrative, and other wordings of the same move count.

## Heading-level slop (C1, C2, C4)

Headlines, subheadlines and mission statements try to describe everything and end up describing nothing.

- C1 Everything Headline: "The All-in-One Platform for Modern Teams"
- C2 Subheadline Repeat: the subheadline restates the headline with more words
- C4 Mission Statement Void: "On a mission to transform how teams collaborate"

Full examples and fixes: [copy/headlines.md](copy/headlines.md)

## Voice and tone slop (C3, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9)

Tone substitutes for substance through fake enthusiasm, passive evasion, fabricated dialogue and unearned emotional intimacy.

- C3 "Great Question!": evaluating the question instead of answering it, including agreement-first openers ("You're absolutely right!")
- C5 Passive Explainer: "It should be noted that...", a passive hedge
- C6 Imaginary Dialogue: "You might be thinking..." with a weak strawman
- C7 Permission Grant: "It's okay to feel overwhelmed", from software
- C8 Aspirational Second Person: "You deserve..." flattery replacing argument
- C9 Offer-and-Sign-off Closer: "Would you like me to...", "Let me know if...", "I hope this helps"

Full examples and fixes: [copy/tone.md](copy/tone.md)
